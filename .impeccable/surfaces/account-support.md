# Account menu, settings and support

MODE: Operate. This extends the existing Asasera interface.
AUTHORITY: User, 7 September 2026: signed-in users must have a circle profile with a dropdown and all basic account actions; forgot/reset password must work; the landing contact form must send to support@asasera.com. Earlier user instruction pins Kahoot colors and styles. This supersedes the old non-dropdown and mailto-only scope decisions.
THESIS: Every signed-in user can identify their account, reach their real role workspace, maintain their profile, recover access and contact support.
OWN-WORLD: Existing purple identity, neutral working canvas, Montserrat/Neo Sans Arabic, compact 4px controls, 8px containers, blue tactile primary actions. Circle avatar uses the saved name's first grapheme or a neutral SVG person.
STORY: Open the profile circle → choose a working account or workspace destination → complete the task with a saved/result state. Support: write → validate → provider acceptance or a recoverable failure preserving the text.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Visible profile circle in the teacher, student and public signed-in headers. Settings begins with the account title and editable name. Support begins with a concise invitation and labelled fields; phones stack the intro and form without horizontal overflow.
FORM: Pinned continuation of the existing system; no new-world roll, concept selection or generated comp. Quality bar is the existing DESIGN.md and student-account surface. No claim of current Kahoot pixel parity.

## Implemented surface

- One shared AccountControl: role-specific workspace links; Account settings; Reset password; Contact support; Sign out. Arrow keys, Home/End, Escape, outside click, route-change dismissal and focus return are implemented. Menu scrolls within the available viewport height.
- `/account`: display-name updates saved through the authenticated backend; email verification status and role; subject/workplace or study-level edit route; emailed password reset; support; existing deliberate account-deletion dialog.
- `/forgot`: current account email prefills without overwriting typing. Existing actual reset-token flow remains responsible for changing the password and revoking refresh sessions.
- Landing `#departments` and `/contact` share one support form. It validates name/email/message lengths, supports Arabic and English, uses a honeypot, blocks simultaneous submits, preserves input on failure, and shows success only after the API confirms provider acceptance. A direct support email link remains available.
- Anonymous public header stays fixed; signed-in public header is in sticky normal flow beneath the verification banner. Signed-in settings use the neutral background without the public ambient wash.
- `/account` and `/contact` are registered in both React and the production nginx route allowlist. Legacy newsletter and database inbox APIs remain unavailable.

## Recorded visual and interaction contract

The 44px circular profile control uses the saved name's first grapheme with bidirectional isolation, or a neutral SVG person. Its resting fill is raised neutral with a one-pixel line; hover and open states use purple with white content. The dropdown sits 8px below the circle at the inline end, has an 8px container radius and padding, and is capped at the smaller of 288px and the viewport minus 32px. The measured available height leaves a 20px reserve below the trigger with a 120px lower bound; the menu scrolls internally and contains overscroll. Its elevation is `0 8px 24px #0003`.

Identity leads with the name, wrapping email and role. Teacher actions are Workspace, My activities, Assignments and Reports; student actions are Your learning and Join a class. Account settings, Reset password and Contact support follow for both roles. A divider separates Sign out, which becomes disabled with pending wording while leaving. Menu rows have 44px minimum targets, 10px/12px vertical/inline padding, compact bold labels and a raised hover/focus background. Opening focuses the first item, or the last when opened with Arrow Up. Up/Down wrap; Home/End jump. Escape closes and returns focus to the trigger; Tab, outside pointer interaction, focus departure and route changes dismiss it.

Settings are capped at 800px, with a 560px name form and flat sections divided by neutral lines. The profile form, save feedback, email/role details, profile continuation, password recovery, support and deliberate deletion follow in that order. At 640px outer spacing contracts to 24px/20px/48px top/inline/bottom. Support is capped at 1100px: introduction and form occupy .8fr/1.2fr columns with a 64px gap. Name and email share a row. At 700px the main layout and field pair stack; the main gap becomes 32px. Public signed-in contact uses 48px top padding, reduced to 32px on phones, beneath the header in normal sticky flow. Anonymous contact retains the fixed-header offsets of 152px and 128px.

Local type follows the existing hierarchy: fluid 28–40px page headings, 24px settings/result headings, 16px fields, 14px bold labels and menu actions, and 12px metadata/hints. The 18px avatar initial and support introduction remain intentional local sizes; the two detector notices were advisory, with no recorded hierarchy or fit defect. They do not add a shared typography token.

Inputs use visible labels, 48px minimum height, 4px corners and one neutral border. Invalid fields pair a danger border with written errors. Support focuses the first invalid field, disables inputs while pending, preserves the draft on failure and renders a bordered neutral result panel after API success. Its visible recipient is support@asasera.com, and the direct mail link remains available. Settings show saved status until the next name edit. Light/dark surfaces, text, borders and danger colors inherit existing tokens; keyboard focus is 3px with a 2px offset, becoming white in dark mode. The dark contact focus measurement confirms that state. Names/messages use automatic direction, email inputs remain LTR, and identity output uses bidirectional isolation. RTL alignment and long email wrapping stay contained. The menu adds no animation; form actions retain shared button and reduced-motion behavior.
